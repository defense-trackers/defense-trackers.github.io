AI Search beta

Ask in plain English. A small language model runs entirely in your browser via WebGPU, grounded in all tracked records — no query ever leaves your machine, and answers cite their sources.

First use downloads a ~1 GB open-weight model (Llama-3.2-1B) into your browser cache. Requires a WebGPU browser (recent desktop Chrome or Edge). Everything runs locally.

For instant keyword search across everything (works in any browser, no download), use the search box on the homepage.

Model: Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct (US-origin, open weights) served via MLC WebLLM. Answers are grounded in the tracked records and cite sources — always verify against the linked source. No server, no telemetry, no data leaves your browser.
